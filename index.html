Research & Publication

Collaborative research from Penn State. The manuscript below was submitted to DAC 2027 in 2026; descriptions emphasize my technical contributions and benchmark work.

Constraint-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Chiplet Placement

Submitted to DAC 2027 · Co-author (third listed author) · 2026

Manuscript: “Constraint-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Joint Thermal and Wirelength Optimization in Chiplet Placement.”

Varun Darshana Parekh, Anusha Devulapally, Ajay Krishna Devulapally, Srivatsa Srinivasa, Mirco Sciulli, Vijaykrishnan Narayanan.

I developed a graph-based, legality-aware reinforcement-learning environment for 2.5D chiplet placement using PPO, imitation learning, action masking, routing-aware wirelength evaluation, and thermal simulation.

Our benchmark experiments demonstrated up to 27% lower wirelength and 58× faster time-to-solution than simulated annealing. I also evaluated zero-shot placement on chiplet configurations unseen during training.

Reinforcement Learning · PPO · Imitation Learning · GATv2 · Python · PyTorch · Thermal Simulation · MILP

Oscillator-Based Ising Machines & GNN Freeze Masks

Combinatorial Optimization · Max-Cut · Parallel Computing

Developed and evaluated GNN-assisted node-freezing strategies for oscillator-based Ising optimization. The workflow combined C++/MPI/OpenMP simulation, oscillator stability and flip dynamics, graph features, and Python/PyTorch model evaluation.

Across seven external MQLib graphs, the methods reduced runtime by 33.8% on average (up to 54.1%) with 0.122% median Max-Cut loss relative to the no-freeze baseline.

C++ · MPI · OpenMP · PyTorch · GNNs · Optimization · Benchmarking
